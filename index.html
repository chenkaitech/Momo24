How to play

Simple rules, satisfying decisions. Make the equation, then make the board work for you.

Pick four numbers

Tap four tiles on the 6×6 board.

Make exactly 24

Add operators and parentheses. Each selected number must appear once in the expression.

Clear and chain

Submit the correct expression, earn linked bonuses, and use the new board to plan your next clear.

Made for thoughtful play

Your progress stays on your device

Level progress, unfinished rounds, preferences, scores, and achievements are stored locally. Game Center can sync eligible scores and achievements when you opt in; it does not back up your full game save.

No ads or tracking SDKs

This version has no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics SDK, or developer-operated gameplay server. Game Center is provided by Apple and is optional.
