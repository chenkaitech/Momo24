Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Data on your device

The game stores progress, scores, achievements, preferences and unfinished rounds on your device. It does not require a developer account.

Optional Game Center

If you choose to sign in, Apple Game Center handles your player identity, eligible daily scores and achievements. A player identifier and pending updates are stored locally to retry syncing for the correct account. Apple processes Game Center data under its own privacy policy. Core gameplay remains available without signing in.

Advertising and analytics

This version includes no advertising SDK, tracking SDK, third-party analytics SDK or developer-operated gameplay server. Purchasing the app is handled by Apple; the game does not receive your payment card details.

Deleting data

Deleting the app removes its local game data. Offloading the app retains data. Apple device backups may retain or restore data. Deleting the app does not erase scores or achievements held by Game Center; manage Game Center through Apple account settings and Apple support.

Contact and support

CXY Studio operates this game. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you send, and use it to respond to your request. Avoid sending passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.
