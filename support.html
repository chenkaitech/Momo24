Momo 24: Math Chain Puzzle — Support

Make 24, clear connected numbers and build chain reactions.

How to play

  1. Select four tiles on the 6×6 board.
  2. Use +, −, ×, ÷ and parentheses to make exactly 24. Each selected tile is used once.
  3. Submit your expression to clear the tiles. Connected selections earn a bonus; matching-number cascades can follow.
  4. Complete the level goal within the move limit. Replay to improve your stars.

Hints and the daily leaderboard

A hint reduces the next successful clear’s base score to 60 and resets the combo. Clearing the expression, undoing or shuffling does not remove that penalty. Only completed 10-move daily rounds with no hints qualify for the daily leaderboard. Level scores stay local. The daily board follows your device’s calendar date.

Game Center

Signing in is optional. Open Settings to sign in, view the leaderboard or view achievements. An internet connection and an Apple Game Center account are needed to sync. Pending scores and achievements retry after reconnecting. Core play works offline.

Progress and sound

Unfinished rounds and progress save on this device. Reinstalling may remove your local progress; Game Center does not back up full game saves. Sound, music and haptics can be changed in Settings. Check Silent Mode if you cannot hear sound.
